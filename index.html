Tiny Dragon

two derivatives of the original 185-byte tiny chess

Legal →
582 bytes
Adds real move-legality checking: correct shapes for every piece, blocked-path detection, no self-capture. Illegal moves are rejected instead of just happening.
Ultra →
181 bytes
Same game, 4 bytes smaller: skips the redundant per-cell attribute write, since BIOS's mode-set already clears the screen to the right color.
Hyper →
174 bytes
Smaller still: also drops the video mode-set call, assuming the screen is already in 80×25 text mode. Fine in a fresh DOSBox/js-dos session; a bigger assumption elsewhere.
